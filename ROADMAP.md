# Roadmap

What is planned, and nothing else. Neither item is built yet.

1. **Evolution across all levels.** Today the flies are tuned and judged on World 1-1 only. The goal is evolution (of the dials only, never the wiring) that works across the levels,
   starting with 1-2, where the goal is a pipe, not a flag.
2. **A lightweight browser version.** Pick a level and fly settings, watch the run. No downloads and no evolution.
