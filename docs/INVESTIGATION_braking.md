## 1. Answer
- **NO: an honest innate brake does not exist in this wiring.** The route exists in the anatomy (LPLC2 -> GABA cell AVLP531 -> DNp09) but is 12% as strong as the direct excitatory LPLC2 -> DNp09 link, so net looming drive onto DNp09 is positive at every hop count, at every drive 3-100, in the real net and in the probe. Measured, real, walking on: DNp09 never goes below its paired no-loom value (min change -0.00 x walk threshold; best observed fall needed 2.6 x, i.e. -72%).
- **GF (DNp01) has no route to DNp09**: 0 direct synapses; 2-hop signed gain -2.1e-6 (about 0.2% of DNp09 rest drive even with both GFs firing at spike amplitude), 1000x too small.
- The committed network already contains the intermediates (242 of 248 inhibitory partner bodies with >= 5 synapses; the other 6 bodies carry 1.2% of the inhibitory synapses). Adding them changes nothing (probe below). So this is not a cut artefact.

## 3. What this means for the plan
- **Braking (D5, E1/E7).** Settles it as phase 6 (a) said, now at full-graph scope and with the dynamic check on the full inhibitory set: the 13/20 first-Goomba deaths cannot be rescued by an innate looming-triggered slow-down. Rules out any "looming releases B" claim and any controller that models it. A brake would have to come from the world side (Goomba speed or approach distance, allowed by the rule) or be declared an invented mechanism (threshold on DNp09, not derivable).
- The inhibition is there in the wiring but is tuned, if anything, to the visual channel LC11 / LC9 / LC31a (hop-2 GABA mass 1.7-4.4e-3), not to LC4 / LPLC2; those types have net positive reach to DNp09 too. If a stop-sensitive stimulus is wanted, the least-bad candidate is LC11 (documented for visual stopping in walking flies: Tanaka & Clark 2020, abstract only) but its net reach to DNp09 is zero, not negative, in the model; it would be an experiment for a later phase, not a derived brake.
- No scalar derived or added. The six missing bodies need no network change.

## 4. Task 2: the 60 deg elevation cap (`ELEVATION_LIMIT_DEG`, invented, range [10, 90])
| claim | source | conf. |
|---|---|---|
| A single LPLC2 has a receptive field of at most 60 deg diameter ("determined 60 deg as the upper bound for an individual LPLC2's receptive field size"). This is a per-cell RF, not a population limit; 60 diameter = +-30 about each cell's centre, not a half-range of 60 for the visual field. | Klapoetke et al. 2017 Nature (PMC7457385, full text via fetch) | verified (full text) |
| VPNs have RFs of 20-40 deg "that together cover visual space"; LC4 RF about 40 deg; behavioural looming discs were presented at **45 deg elevation** and still drove the behaviour (a stimulus at 45 would have weight 0.25 under the current function). | Dombrovski et al. 2023 Nature (PMC9849133, fetch summary) | verified (summary of text) |
| LPLC2 densely tiles visual space (about 80 cells per optic lobe); LC4 and LPLC2 synapse directly on the GF. Nothing on elevation. | Ache 2019 / von Reyn 2017 abstract-level, VFB snippet | abstract only |
| Nern et al. 2025: retinotopic, "uniform coverage" mentioned; no degree values for any VPN type or the eye | PMC12119369 (fetch summary) | abstract only |
| Wu et al. 2016 (eLife 21022): fetch failed, nothing verified | - | not verified |
| Drosophila eye vertical extent; interommatidial angle (about 4.5-5 deg) | no source found / memory | from memory, not used |
| MaleCNS: no ROI or dendrite coordinates for LC4/LPLC2 in the available files (annotation columns: somaLocation only; assignedOlHex1/2 exist only for medulla columnar types such as Mi1 (1,762), Tm1/Tm2/Tm4/Tm9/Tm20, L1-L5, C2/C3, none for LC4, LPLC2, LC10a). Proxy used: input-weighted mean column of each cell's hex-labelled presynaptic partners (Tm4/Tm20 for LPLC2, Tm2/Tm4 for LC4: 5% and 12% of their input synapses; median spread 1.3-1.9 columns). Result: the 182 LPLC2 and 126 LC4 centres span hex1 3.7-34, hex2 2.4-37 against the Mi1 grid 1-36 x 1-39; 91-97% of Mi1 columns lie within 3 columns of an LPLC2 or LC4 centre (left and right optic lobe separately), 100% within 5. | `p11b_hex.py` | [M] with a caveat: hex axes are not calibrated to elevation here |

**Best-supported statement.** LC4 and LPLC2 populations tile the **whole** eye (data: columns; literature: "densely cover visual space"); neither source supports a retinal-elevation cut-off at 60 deg, nor any linear fall-off `1 - elev/limit`. The per-cell RF (60 deg diameter at most) is the only hard literature number, and it describes a cell, not a visual-field edge. What the weight function actually does in the sim is attenuate theta for objects off Mario's row, a proxy for "not a threat", which no source here establishes. Dombrovski 45 deg looming working argues against a 75% attenuation at 45 deg.
**Recommendation.** Keep it declared **invented** (not derivable): the elevation half-range cannot be converted from columns to degrees from these files, and no verified full-text source gives it. If a derived replacement is wanted without a scalar, use geometry: theta = 2 atan(w / (2 sqrt(dx^2 + dy^2))) (the object's true distance includes the vertical offset), zero new scalars; flagged as a suggestion, untested here.

## 5. Open questions
- Glutamate sign: GluCl is common but not universal; recomputing with Glu = 0 raises DNp09 reach, does not create a brake (for LPLC2 and LC4, GABA carries 95% and 63% of the inhibitory 2-hop mass).
- Sign 0 for 46,000 bodies could hide further inhibition; DNp09's own unsigned input is 3.5%, so the direct view is nearly complete, the 2-3 hop view is not.
- 3 noise seeds, open loop (distance follows a fixed trace); ReLU at DNp09 means inhibition below zero is invisible, but walk is at 3.66 x threshold so it is not near zero.
- Cached edges are >= 2 syn; DNp09 inputs and GF outputs were also counted at >= 1.
- Hex proxy covers 5-12% of input synapses; dendrite extent in the lobula is not available.
