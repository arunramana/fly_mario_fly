## 1. Answer

1. **The mushroom body is already in the committed 16,464-neuron network** (KC 4,064, MBON 97, DAN 340 are whole populations of the manifest, and APL and DPM are on-path interior neurons). The "probe" with named PAM/PPL1/PPL2/APL/DPM populations has a weight matrix bit-identical to the committed one, so k=3/N=5, speed (0.83 ms/step), calibration and both gates are unchanged.
2. **Taste reaches dopamine neurons, but weakly and non-specifically.** Pharyngeal taste neurons at 100 Hz raise PAM by +0.168 Hz (rest 0.0072 Hz, rest-noise sd 4e-5), about 1.7e-3 Hz per Hz of drive. The route is only 2.7-3.8x a random olfactory control, PPL1 rises with it (0.44x of PAM), labellar neurons are no better than the control, and degree-shuffled wiring gives 3.9-25x more dopamine than the real wiring. Sugar vs bitter is not annotated. Pain: no nociceptor class exists; thermosensory neurons are the only punishment surrogate and they do not separate PAM from PPL1.
3. **Conditioning works at the mushroom-body level and stops there.** With a learning rate set so the CS+ response of reward-targeted MBONs halves (lr 1.0, invented), the cue-specific response falls to 0.45x (control cue 0.76x; fixed weights 1.000x). Even so, the change reaches the motor neurons at at most 0.05% of any motor threshold (0.02% with oracle dopamine). A learned change would need at least 200x amplification to matter.
4. **The depression-only rule drifts one way** under every schedule with dopamine (reward only, punishment only, balanced, random), and sensed punishment does not depress a different MBON group than sensed reward.
5. **Recommendation: do not build E4/E5 as behaviour experiments.** At most build the mechanism-level conditioning demo of section 4 (cheap, honest, labelled "does not reach the motor map"). Details and decisions in section 5.

## 3. What this means for the plan

- **D6/D7 closed on the MB side.** No change to k=3, N=5; the whole MB is already in; extra named populations (PAM, PPL1, PPL2, APL, DPM) are free (no new neurons, no new edges, hash changes only through the rule text). If the tool wants to *show* them, add names to the manifest, then re-stamp, not recalibrate. Caveat: N=5 removes 91% of PAM->KC synapses (the real site of dopamine action on KC terminals), so any dopamine route must go through DAN->MBON, as in the legacy design. That is a modelling choice, not the biology.
- **Mechanism "sweet taste" cannot be labelled "real route, specific".** Honest label: real wiring exists (GRN -> 2 interior hops -> PAM), amplitude about 0.1-0.2% of drive, pharyngeal taste neurons best (2.7-3.8x control), sugar identity unannotated, and the drive amplitude is invented. Plan row 3.3 "Sweet taste" stays "invented drive on real wiring".
- **Mechanism "pain" is ruled out** (no nociceptor annotation; thermo does not separate PPL1 from PAM). E5 as written cannot pass the rule honestly.
- **The learning rule, as built, fails its own sanity tests under sensed dopamine**: one-way drift, no valence separation for punishment, KC code not sparse, cue self-reinforcement (ORN -> PPL1/PAM), tonic PAM from the invented home landmark (b6) that would depress eligible synapses from the first frame of every episode.
- **E4 has no headroom**: E3 is already 20/20 innately; E4's pass criterion "touch rate rises" cannot be met.

## 4. What learning could and could not change on 1-1

- Could (in principle): shift an odour-driven MBON response and, through MBON26/27/31/32, DNa02 and MDN (steer, back). Measured size at the outputs: <= 0.05% of the motor thresholds. It cannot change the Giant Fiber (no direct MBON->GF; max linear gain 4.8e-4) and so cannot make the fly jump earlier or at the pipe.
- Reward signal in training scenes: none in 1-1 (never touches a mushroom, block, coin or stomp). E3-like scenes (mushroom placed) give one contact event per episode. The honest sensed reward is not taste at the mouth (Mario has none): it is an invented object characteristic ("the mushroom tastes of sugar", entering at head GRN), allowed by the rule only if labelled invented.
- Cannot: (i) Goomba deaths (13/20) are GF timing against distance; (ii) the pipe stall (7/20) needs a sensed obstacle and an A press, not a value; (iii) no cue reaches KCs from LC4/LPLC2 (visual cues need an optic-lobe-to-KC route not in this network); (iv) learning cannot create a punishment signal.
- **Honest answer: nothing useful at the behaviour level with this wiring and these thresholds.**

## 5. Recommendation and decisions

**Do not build E4/E5 as behaviour experiments.** If a learning deliverable is wanted, build only a "mechanism demo": odour cue + pharyngeal taste (invented, labelled), real vs 5 shuffles vs fixed weights, outcome = CS+ MBON response, reported with the line "motor change <= 0.05% of threshold". About one session, no game runs.

Decisions for Arun:
1. Accept that E4/E5 become mechanism-level (MBON response) results or are dropped. Recommended: drop E5, keep a demo for E4.
2. Taste channel: pharyngeal only (best route, unverified sugar) or all head GRN; the drive (100 here) is invented.
3. Allow an MBON-to-motor gain (needs >= 200x)? Recommended no: no physiology to calibrate it against, and the sensed effect would be the gain.
4. Rule form: depression-only (legacy, drifts) vs bidirectional order-dependent (Handler 2019). Needs a sparse KC code first.
5. Tonic PAM from the home landmark (and pursuit) must be gated or accepted before any plasticity runs.
6. lr (1.0, set here to halve the CS+ response on the real network) and routing (DAN->MBON weights) are invented; list them in `brain/scalars.py` if built.

## 6. Open questions and limits

- One odour pair, 8 trials, one noise-seed set for plasticity, rate model (no spikes); the 0.45 CS+ ratio is by construction of lr, the unconstructed results are generalisation (0.76), drift, valence split, shuffles and motor reach.
- `d_j` through DAN->MBON weights; a KC-side (DAN->KC compartment) rule was not tested and would keep only 9% of the PAM->KC synapses at N=5.
- Which MaleCNS gustatory types are sweet/bitter (Engert 2022 used morphology and connectivity in FAFB; not mapped). Whether the unlabelled sensory types that reach PPL1 (SAxx02, ENS5, GNG643 in Phase 0) are nociceptors.
- Whether LC10a->PAM is biological (model only; not checked in literature).
- A larger cue drive (30 instead of 3) scales the motor effect about 10x (linear), still about 0.5% of threshold.
