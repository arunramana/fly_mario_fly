## 1. Answer

- **(a) Looming does not reduce or stop walking; it only triggers the GF.** In the real wiring DNp09 goes up, not down (paired change vs no-loom 0.00 to +0.85 x walk threshold), steer and back move < 0.14 x threshold, B stays pressed 100% of frames. No loom-type neuron tested (8 types) lowers DNp09, and the only stop/halt neurons named in the data (bluebell DNg60) move < 5%. **No innate route to stopping.**
- **(b) The new-path fly senses nothing at a static pipe.** If the pipe is rendered as a growing face at LC4/LPLC2 it fires the GF 40 px before the face (first A ~32-35 px) and drives A to 2.5 x threshold; shuffles do not fire. At LC10a there is no route to GF/TTMn/A (GF membrane <= 0.23 of threshold, A <= 0.17): it only moves walk/steer. Pipe 1 (32 px) needs only a 1-5 frame hold if take-off is within ~29-41 px; pipes of 48-64 px need holds of ~6-12 frames.
- **(c) PSI and every wing motor neuron exist in MaleCNS; only PSI is in the committed network.** The GF reaches PSI by 16 chemical synapses (0.26% of PSI input), and PSI's neurotransmitter is "unclear", so its outputs carry no weight. DLMn/wing MNs respond (x6-8 baseline) through interior cells, only as long as the GF/TTMn burst (0-1 frame difference), and no more than random wiring. **No honest route to a graded or sustained jump drive.**

## 3. What this means for the plan

- **Goomba while walking (D5, E1/E7).** The 13/20 deaths in the baseline are GF timing against a 3.5 px/frame closing speed (Phase baseline replay); the real wiring offers no stop to buy time. Looming adds to DNp09 (LPLC2 -> DNp09 is direct), which the motor map reads as run. Zacarias 2018 says that same neuron gives freezing at low speed; the model has no state-dependent freeze and none can be justified from wiring. No mechanism to enable; what it rules out is "looming makes the fly stop" and any E1/E7 claim that depends on it.
- **E2a (pipes).** The mechanism the plan calls "expanding obstacle" has a route (looming entry) and a timing that is borderline-feasible for pipe 1 only. It does not exist in the new path (no obstacle perception; the only `tile_ram` use is legacy). Pipes 2-4 (48-64 px) need holds of ~6-12 frames, above what the closed-loop A-run distribution gives (median 4, max 8), so a pass on 1-1 beyond pipe 1 is not expected. LC10a gives no jump route; a lateralised "visual cue" at LC10a would steer, not jump.
- **Jump height (Phase 3b decision E stays).** PSI and wing MNs give no graded/sustained drive: their bursts track the GF/TTMn burst, the GF-PSI link is invisible in chemical counts, PSI's sign is missing. A hop-height law cannot be derived from wiring; option A (one GF spike = N frames) remains the only motivated alternative and still carries an invented N.
- **D1 (vision tier):** unchanged. **D3/D5:** E2a may be run for pipe 1 only; E2b (gaps) not examined here. **D7:** MaleCNS does contain PSI, DLMn and all wing MNs, leg tactile and campaniform cells; none are in the sensory/output tiers.

## 5. Decisions for Arun
1. Accept "no stopping route" and keep the Goomba-while-walking failure as the baseline fact (recommended), or change the world side (e.g. Goomba speed or approach distance, allowed by the rule but it changes 1-1, not the fly).
2. Port an obstacle perception (looming of visible solid-tile faces) as E2a, scoped to pipe 1, with a pass criterion that does not assume pipes 2-4; or leave E2a as an honest fail. Say whether a static pipe counts as a looming object (no biological source; flies climb or turn).
3. Whether to add leg tactile/campaniform cells to the sensory tier for contact (changes the network hash, needs recalibration and a new route test).
4. Keep Phase 3b option E (no hold law) and do not add PSI or wing MNs to the motor map (recommended).

## 6. Open questions and limits
- All sims are open loop (Mario's distance follows a fixed 3 px/frame trace); closed-loop elevation weighting and a hop that raises Mario would truncate the burst (Phase 3b). Whether pipe 1 is cleared needs a closed run, not done here.
- Pipe 2-4 clearance is inferred from apex (not run). Hop windows are on a ~6 px grid.
- The loom width-64 trace has an onset artefact (first-frame dtheta from theta0 = 31 deg); ignored.
- LC9's and LC31a's function in vivo and their drive range are not established here; their strong DNp09 reach is a wiring fact, not a behavioural claim.
- Sign of many cells is "unclear" (55,379 bodies without a usable consensus), so signed full-graph reach understates both excitation and inhibition; unsigned reach is also given.
- Probe C is one noise seed per shuffle; probe A and the pipe tests three; the Goomba tests five.
