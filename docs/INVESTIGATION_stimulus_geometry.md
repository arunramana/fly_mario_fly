Date 2026-10-06. Investigation only: no code, config, calibration, manifest, motor map or test changed. Scratch scripts `audit/p12g_a..k.py` (gitignored; raw `audit/p12g_g.json`, `p12g_h_*.txt`). Emulator replays are read-only; the only non-default thing done to the game or the fly is a monkeypatch of `PursuitMechanism.encode` inside scratch processes for the E3 counterfactuals (section 3). Seeds 9300-9305 (non-eval). Network `brain_k3_N5` (16,464 neurons), `brain/calibration.json` unchanged.
Tags: **[C]** read from code; **[M]** measured (RAM / emulator / committed brain); **[I]** inferred. No literature number is used as a target here. The two literature mentions (Ache 2019 GF ~40 deg; Ribeiro 2018 LC10a) are quoted from the repo's own docs and were not re-verified in this sprint.

## 1. Answers (one line per item)
1. **Body width (PARTLY).** Confirmed that Mario is a point at his sprite's left edge [C]. But for an enemy the current d is exactly centre-to-centre (both x are left edges of 16 px sprites) [M]. The size of the effect depends on a convention, not on data: theta = 40 deg is reached at d = 22 now, d = 32 with the hitbox gap (d - 10, contact at theta = 180 deg), d = 38 with the user's sprite-edge convention (d - 16), and d = 23.7 if the hitbox width (10 px) replaces 16 px as w. Lead = 0 to 4.6 frames. The user's 22 -> 38 arithmetic is right; it is not the only defensible convention.
2. **Pipe inversion (CONFIRMED), and it is a weight artefact.** Peak theta 50.2 / 41.1 / 37.7 deg for 32 / 48 / 64 px, reproduced; without the elevation weight the order reverses (97.6 / 119.5 / 132.7 at the stall). Fixing it does remove the pipe-3 silence without touching the 60 deg cap, but it fires at d about 85, earlier than the measured take-off window.
3. **Pursuit azimuth (CONFIRMED, inverted).** `az = 90 * clip(dx/128)` uses the depth axis; touching = 0 deg, 128 px ahead = 90 deg. E3 keys on "small moving object ahead" (symmetric drive reaches 6/6 too); the sign carries ahead/behind and is not decorative (flipped sign: 0/6, presses Left).
4. **Identical hemispheres (CONFIRMED).** Both producers set `{'L': th, 'R': th}`. Under identical drive the two Giant Fibers cross threshold within 1 frame of each other. The looming L/R split is decorative at the stimulus; the wiring fact (invariant 5) is untouched.
5. **dtheta per frame (REFUTED as a risk).** `act` runs once per `env.step`, one NES frame; no frame_skip exists in `brain/`; disc calibration uses the same one-sample-per-16.7 ms convention.

## 3. Item notes

### 3.1 Body width (item 1)
- Mario's x is the left edge of the sprite; his hitbox sits 3 px (small) or 2 px (big) inside it and is 10 or 12 px wide. For an enemy, `rec.x - mario_x` equals the centre-to-centre distance, so for enemies Mario is a *centre* point, not a left-edge point. For obstacles the face is a surface but d is measured from Mario's left edge, 13 px behind his hitbox front: that half is plainly body-blind (wall contact is d = 14, theta clamps at `max(d, 1)`).
- The disc formula 2 atan(l / (v tau)) puts contact at theta = 180 deg. The game's contact (Goomba death) is at d = 9, where the current theta is 83 deg. The calibration therefore assumed 6.1 frames from GF threshold to contact for this l/v; the game gives 3.3 [M, arithmetic].
- Convention is not derivable from the connectome. What the RAM does determine: contact is d = 10 (small) / 11 (big) for a Goomba and d = 13-14 for a wall. A convention that puts theta = 180 deg at contact uses only those measured numbers. The width w (16 sprite vs 10 hitbox) is a separate choice and changes the lead from +4.6 to about -0.5 frames, so w must be fixed first.
- Side findings: `mario_centre_y` and the specs assume Mario is 16 / 32 px tall; the hitbox is 12 / 24 (centre 2 / 4 px lower than the model). E1's `is_contact` uses |dx| < 16 while the game kills at d about 9-10, so the E1 contact frame is early by about 6 px (about 1.7 frames) [C + M].

### 3.2 Pipes and the elevation weight (item 2)
- The weight `1 - elev/60` is applied to the run's centre; a taller run has a higher centre, so for a constant approach the weight falls faster than w grows once d is below about 44 px (pipe 3), producing the inversion. For any face that overlaps Mario's body, extent-based elevation is 0, so the weight is inert and theta = 2 atan(H / 2d).
- The 60 deg cap (`ELEVATION_LIMIT_DEG`) is not touched by any of this. It still acts on enemies, and on an airborne Mario for obstacles, whose body leaves the face (a Mario risen 24 px reads pipe 3 at 132.7 deg, the airborne 70-80 deg peaks of the stall doc).
- The no-weight and 3D variants give the same timing to within 4-5 px for pipes 2 and 3; the 3D version adds the hypotenuse but nothing the weight-free one lacks.
- Caveat for a fix: GF fires far earlier on tall faces (d 85-88 for 64 px). That is what the stimulus geometry says (a 64 px wall at 85 px subtends 41 deg), but it lies outside the measured take-off window, so a corrected cue may produce a different failure (early press) rather than a clear. This is a [I] about outcomes and was not run.

### 3.3 Pursuit azimuth (item 3) and proposed provenance wording (not applied)
- `docs/SCALARS_BRAIN.md`, `LC10A_AZIMUTH_SPLIT` (invented): "Not a visual azimuth. The NES side view has no lateral axis; 'azimuth' is the signed horizontal screen distance dx between Mario and the target, 90 deg * clip(dx / 128, -1, 1) (128 px = half the screen width is a fact; the dx -> angle mapping is invented). dx is the same axis that gives looming its depth, so a target touching Mario reads 0 deg (equal L/R) and one 128 px ahead reads 90 deg (right only). For any target ahead the right share is >= 0.5: the channel carries ahead (Right) vs behind (Left), the same 'ahead = Right' convention as the legacy hemisphere routing, not a measured retinal position. E3 reaches 6/6 with the lateral term removed (az = 0) and 0/6 with its sign flipped (Phase 5b)."
- `LC10A_GAIN` (invented), add: "E3 seed 9300: mean R drive 4.1 Hz, max 8.2 Hz at arousal 0.65."
- `GOAL_CUE_SALIENCE` (invented), add: "A static, right-only drive that is not a visible object (LC10a prefers small moving objects). In B11 (seed 9300, first 446 frames) it is 100% of right-LC10a drive (15.0 Hz every frame; left 0); pursuit contributes nothing until a mushroom is pursued. E3 has no landmark."
- Comment in `brain/perception.py:71` ("a fact of the screen, not tuned") should read "128 px is half the screen width (a fact); treating dx as an azimuth is an invented projection".
- LC10a literature (Ribeiro 2018, Hindmarsh Sten 2021): from the repo's docs, not re-verified here.

### 3.4 Hemispheres (item 4)
- Past claims: SPEC_jump "strict lateralisation" (each detector drives exactly one GF) is a **wiring fact** from MaleCNS and stays true; what the stimulus does with it is symmetric. The legacy audit item S5 (ahead -> R hemisphere, "swapping L/R removes both wins") concerns the legacy path only: the redesign removed that routing (enemies behind skipped, both sides equal). SPEC_brain_core, SPEC_e2a ("both hemispheres get the same value") and SPEC_bio_strict state the symmetry openly. No redesign result (E0, E1, E2a, E3, B11, braking, dose-response) depends on a looming L/R difference [C]; the E1/E2a real-vs-shuffle contrast comes from wiring, not from stimulus lateralisation (shuffles: GF 0/6 under identical drive in 2e, 2 arms). Invariant 5 in CLAUDE.md should say "the wiring is lateralised; the looming stimulus for a frontal object is deliberately symmetric".
- What is not decorative: the motor map's steer (DNa R vs L) and LC10a R vs L; both are lateralised by the pursuit sign and the landmark, which are inventions (3.3).

### 3.5 dtheta (item 5)
- No fix needed. For reference, at the GF spike (d about 22, 3.6 px/frame) theta changes by about 0.09 rad/frame; a harness acting every k frames would multiply it by k, so any future frame_skip must scale or the LC4 drive would no longer match the disc.

## 5. Fix order by risk (nothing applied; revalidation is its own sprint)
1. **Docs only** (3.3, 3.4, comment at perception.py:71, SCALARS_BRAIN, CLAUDE.md invariant 5 wording). Zero behaviour change, no revalidation.
2. **Pursuit az -> 0** (item 3). Touches only the pursuit channel: revalidate E3 (20 held-out seeds, real vs both shuffles, `real_no_pursuit`), B11 (baseline with a mushroom: first real use of pursuit alongside the landmark), E0 unaffected (landmark only), E1 and E2a unaffected (no pursuit). Low risk (E3 already 6/6 with az = 0, 115-117 frames).
3. **Obstacle extent geometry** (item 2). Touches only `ObstacleExpansion` (E2a, B11). Revalidate: `audit/e2a_synth.py` (pipe 1, block, stairs), E2a (20 seeds real vs shuffles), B11, and re-run the stall-x724 replay logic. Expect behavioural change: pipe 3 no longer silent but 10-30 px early; pipe 2 about 20-30 px earlier. Highest effect size on the baseline; do after 2 because its blast radius is smaller than 4 but its outcome is least predictable.
4. **Body width / surface distance** (item 1). Touches every enemy looming (E1, E2a, B11, dose-response) and the obstacle d. Revalidate: disc parity (unchanged, it is synthetic), E1 (20 seeds; first GF 2.9-4.6 frames earlier), E2a, B11, `docs/RESULTS_dose_response.md` runs. Do last and together with the obstacle d shift (2g: d - 13 moves tall faces' GF to 127-130 px) so the pair is revalidated once. Freeze w and the eye position from RAM before running anything.
5. **No action:** dtheta frame convention (item 5); hemisphere symmetry (item 4) except wording.

## 6. Open questions
- Which w for enemies (sprite 16 vs hitbox 10)? It decides whether item 1 is a 0 or a 4.6-frame change; nothing in the connectome or the RAM picks it.
- Whether an early GF on tall faces (d about 85) is survivable was not run (outcome evaluation was out of scope); the stall doc's windows suggest a mismatch, [I].
- Item 1 and item 2 interact (the extent fix at d - 13 fires at 127-130 px for 64 px pipes, theta there 31 deg): a joint decision.
- E3 counterfactuals were 6 seeds, stimulus-only variants, reported as mechanism diagnostics, not as pass criteria.
- Walking speed 2.57 px/frame was taken from the request; this sprint measured running 3.0 (xspeed 48/16) and the Goomba at 0.62 px/frame.
- The class-preserving shuffle was not run on the synthetic approaches (degree shuffles only, 2 seeds).
