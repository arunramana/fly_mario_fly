# M2: the mushroom BEHIND Mario: sweet smell (left) vs home (right) (base 4db1eb3; HUM_GAIN frozen; SMELL_GAIN set once and frozen)

**Result: with the smell on, the real fly turns back and eats the mushroom (frame 64, x 569); with the smell off he never turns and the mushroom reaches him only because he is stopped at pipe 2 (frame 88, x 594). The turn comes late (only after he is blocked at pipe 2) and the steering margin is razor thin (2e-5 over the Left threshold at saturation), but with noise (sigma 0.05, 5 seeds) he still turns and eats it 5/5.**

## 2. SMELL_GAIN, chosen once and frozen
- Level at which the steer crosses -0.01 (left antenna, HOME_R on, steady state; bisection): **49.7706**.
- The mushroom 64 px behind him: Mario at x 560 (feet 176), eye (572, 160); mushroom 16 x 16 on the ground at x 496, centre (504, 168): d = 68.469 px, theta = 2 atan(8 / 68.469) = **0.23263 rad**.
- **SMELL_GAIN = 49.7706 / 0.23263 = 213.9495** (frozen in `Knobs`). So at exactly 64 px the input is at the crossing; nearer is stronger, farther is weaker (the ORN activity is clipped at 50, so beyond level ~50 nothing changes).

## Paragraph
The real ORN -> PN -> LAL030 / VES / AVLP -> DNa wiring can overcome the home signal: with the gain set once from the steering threshold, the fly turns back (Left alone) for the mushroom behind him and eats it, the controls without the smell (or with shuffled wiring) do not, and 5/5 noisy runs agree. Three honest qualifiers: (1) he only turns after pipe 2 stops him, because at 1.5 px/frame against the mushroom's 1 px/frame the gap grows and the smell weakens below the crossing while he walks; the gain was set for 64 px and the scene starts at 64; (2) the steer saturates at -0.01002 against a threshold of -0.01, a margin of 2e-5 on this pruned brain (the full 235-neuron set had 4e-4), so "turn back" is on a knife edge by construction; (3) the smell-off fly also eats the mushroom (by contact at the pipe), only later (frame 88 vs 64), so eaten-or-not alone is a weak readout: the Left presses and the earlier frame are the smell's signature. Also fixed on the way: nes-py's `reset()` restores a stored backup, so a hand-over snapshot must not be stored (the first controls ran from a corrupted state and were re-run from a clean one; the reported numbers are from the clean runs).
