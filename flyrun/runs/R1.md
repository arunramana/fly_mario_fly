## What does not fit / is approximate (to look at in the stills)
1. **JO and ORN have no soma in the MaleCNS** (sensory neurons; 184 of the 881 have no `somaLocation`: 20 JO, 163 ORN, 1 other). They are drawn as schematic clusters above the head (JO nearer the midline, ORN wider, split by side), not at real positions; the panel says so in small print.
2. **The brain panel is top-heavy**: the brain occupies the upper ~300 px of the 1036-px column, the nerve cord (where TTMn sits) the rest; the lower half is nearly empty and dim. (Could be scaled / cropped; left as the true proportions.)
3. **Labels collide a little**: "Giant Fiber (DNp01)" and "DNp09 walk" sit close together (both neurons are almost at the same place) and a leader line crosses the Giant Fiber label.
4. **Hum and smell are zero in arm A**, so the green clusters and the green traces are flat in the stills; they will only come alive in arm C.
5. **The home circle** sits over game content at the right edge (visible on the castle in the flag still).
6. **Steer trace scale**: with home on the steer sits near +0.05, so the +-0.01 lines are in the lower half and the +0.01 label is hidden behind the line.
7. **Still 1** is "no threat ahead" by the fly's own sense; a stomped Goomba is on screen behind him.
8. The left-right flip: the fly's LEFT is drawn on the viewer's left (the raw x axis has L at the larger x), so the right side of the brain (home, looming detectors) is on the right, as in the game.
