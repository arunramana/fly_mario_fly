# E2: evolution Stage 2, all the knobs that are choices, 1-1 only (pre-registered in `runs/E2_prereg.md`, commit 3f9f456; 16 genes, the E1s best as the start)

**Result: E2 is no better than E1s. The search never found a candidate that beat its own start genome (the E1s best, training fitness 1.171): the best candidate of any generation was generation 6 at 1.118 (11 of 12 training flags), and the population mean stayed at 0.19-0.55 (most 16-gene perturbations break the fly). On the pre-registered test the E2 best ties E1s on HOLDOUT flags (10/10 both) and is worse on the score (1480 vs 2275: 2.0 vs 2.7 blocks, 0 vs 8 mushrooms), and on the pre-registered HARDER set it has fewer flags (3/20 vs 5/20); on the extra set of the amendment it is 10/20 against E1s's 11/20. The "best" of E2 (a generation-6 candidate) is worse than the start it was started from.** The pre-registered harder set is mostly a harness artifact (see section 4), which does not change that comparison.

## 1. Bounds
As in `E2_prereg.md` (16 genes, 5 drives + headroom on, the 907 brain; E1s genes with their floors; G, ALPHA, CLIP, DNP09_THRESH +-50%; the four geometry genes on ranges: PIT_TOWER_POS [0, 1], PIT_TOWER_H [0.5, 1.5], HUM_REACH_PX [48, 80], EYE_DY [0, 16]). Population = the CMA default for 16 genes = 12; <= 40 generations, patience 10; timing 10.1 s per flag episode, projection <= 1.2 h, the full budget used; the search ran **17 generations (stopped by the patience rule), ~21 min**.

## 6. Verdict
Evolving the choices on top of the E1s genes did not help: no candidate beat the start on the training set, the test ties or loses against the E1s best on every number (HOLDOUT score 1480 vs 2275, hard-set flags 3/20 vs 5/20, amendment set 10/20 vs 11/20), and the 1-2 opening is worse. The honest reading is that the E1s genome is already near a plateau on the training set that this search (12 candidates per generation in 16 dimensions, 17 generations) could not improve on, and that with 16 genes most random candidates break the fly.
