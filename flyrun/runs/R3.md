## JO / ORN positions
MaleCNS has no soma for the 20 JO and 163 ORNs, and the local data has no synapse coordinates either (the weights file is body-to-body counts only; no skeletons). So each is drawn at the **synapse-weighted mean position of the somata of its postsynaptic partners that lie in the brain** (z < 50k), streamed once from the 152M-row weights file (`flyrun/render/sensory.py`, ~10 s, cached in `tutorials/_cache/sensory_pos.npz`): JO -> the AMMC neurons they feed, ORNs -> the PNs / LNs of DM1, VA2, DM4. All 184 neurons without a soma (the 183 JO / ORN and one relay) got a position (0 undefined; x 37k-62k, z 12.7k-24k voxels, i.e. inside the upper brain, nothing outside it). This is a proxy for the synapse centroid, not the synapse centroid; the footnote on the panel says "shown at their brain terminals".

## What did not fit / is approximate
1. **The nerve cord is drawn at about half the brain's scale** (0.0034 vs 0.0070 px/voxel): at the brain's scale the cord alone would be ~550 px tall and the text block and the 10-row index would not fit under it. TTMn is two small pink-red dots in it.
2. **The index has no room for more than ~10 rows** (rows end at y 1068 of 1080); the relays without a row are neutral.
3. **Brightness is per neuron against its own run maximum with a floor of 0.02**: without the floor the ~1e-3 baseline of the smell-path relays looked fully lit in arms without smell; with it the ORN row bar is not shown full either (the ORN row counts the receptor neurons only; the relays share the colour but not the value).
4. **The JO / ORN dots sit at partner-soma centroids**, so they land in the upper-middle of the brain (JO pink at the top centre, ORN yellow-green in a band across the top), not at the glomeruli themselves.
5. **No visible Giant Fiber ring or "JUMP" flash** in this layout (none was specified); the Giant Fiber shows as a brighter, larger orange dot and its index bar. 
6. The home dot is 50% opaque and looks light-grey on the sky.
7. The arm / frame text next to "LIVE BRAIN" and the brightness line are small additions to the requested text block.

## R3b: text, index rates, finer somata (approved on `clips/R3_stills/R3b_armC_216.png`)
- Text block: "brightness = activity"; the footnote is one line, "JO/ORN at their brain terminals (cell bodies in the antenna)".
- Index: the JO and ORN rows already showed their neurons' RATE (mean smoothed `h` of the 20 JO / 163 ORNs, like every other row), not the input level, with the same bar rule (group mean against its own run maximum); unchanged. The values (JO 9.3, ORN 26.5 in arm C frame 216) are large because the sensory neurons are driven directly by the input.
- Finer somata: the grey background somata are 1-px points at low alpha (15 grey levels per soma, additive, capped at 70); the circuit neurons have a ~2 px core (radius 1.0, 1.8 for DNp01 / TTMn / DNp09 / DNa, growing by half with activity) and a halo of sigma 2.5 (was 5) at gain 1.2 (was 1.5). The nerve cord is left as is (fainter because it has fewer somata at its scale).
- Videos (60 fps, 1920 x 1080, gitignored): `clips/R3_armA_full.mp4` (arm A, noop 0, sigma 0, the flag run: 1947 frames = 32.5 s, 48 MB) and `clips/R3_armC_full.mp4` (arm C, noop 0, sigma 0, dies at x 1220: 760 frames = 12.7 s, 19 MB).
