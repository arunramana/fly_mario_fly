# M2b: smell-only, and a mushroom moving AWAY (base 7dbcf7a, 881-neuron brain; SMELL_GAIN 213.9495 and HUM_GAIN 14.5342 frozen; no new gains)

**Result: smell alone (home off) is a strong, sustained drive: he turns toward the mushroom at once and eats it (frame 38 vs frame 78 with both off). With home on and a mushroom moving away, home wins from frame 4: no turn, no chase (0/5 with noise). With home off he chases a mushroom moving away, but cannot catch it: the screen never scrolls back, so he is stopped at its left edge after ~95 frames while the mushroom keeps going and despawns (0/5 eaten with noise too).**

## 1. Scene A: the M2 setup, HOME off (smell ON; home off, looming off, hum off; `runs/M2b_A_*.csv`)
Hand-over as M2: Mario x 560, mushroom x 496 moving right.
- **He moves, and only because of the smell**: DNp09 stays ~0 (mean 0.0007: no walk drive without the home signal); the button comes from the steering path alone: **Left (alone) from frame 0 for 41 frames**. Steer (DNa R-L): -0.0245 at frame 0, -0.0626 from frame 5, minimum -0.0637: **margin beyond the -0.01 threshold 0.054** (with home on it was 0.00002).
- He first drifts 11 px right (momentum from the previous frames' scripted walk), then goes left: **turned back at gap 65 px; eaten at frame 38, Mario x 544; power-up 0 -> 1.**
- Control (smell off, home off): he stands (steer 0, DNp09 0; 25 px of momentum drift), and the mushroom reaches him by contact at frame 78, x 585. So the smell makes him eat it 40 frames sooner and by walking to it.

## 2. Scene B: the mushroom moving AWAY (looming off, hum off; `runs/M2b_B*.csv`)
**Method.** The scripted driver (enemies removed) takes Mario over pits 1 and 2 to the flat stretch x 1424-2144 and hands over on the ground at **x 1700**; a mushroom is then **created by RAM in the power-up slot** (slot 5: active flag, type 0x2E, state 128, y 184 = on the ground) at x 1636, **64 px behind him, moving LEFT** (direction 2, speed -16 = 1 px/frame). I tested it: it slides left at 1 px/frame and falls where the floor ends (the eat-by-contact of an object made this way was not separately tested; the right-moving M2 mushroom was eaten normally). Nothing stops him on the right for 440 px (the next obstacle is the staircase at x 2144), so the old problem (the pipe stopping him) is gone. (The natural pipe-1 bounce leaves only ~110 px of free ground ahead, so I used the RAM creation; reported as asked.)

- **B1 is the real test of smell against home, and smell loses**: at the start the input equals the 64-px crossing level, but he walks away at 1.5 px/frame while the mushroom does too, the gap grows and the smell level falls (49.8 -> 17 by frame 48), so the steer returns to +0.016. Without a stronger smell (or a weaker home) he never turns back for a retreating mushroom.
- **B2 turns back immediately and strongly**, so it is the smell that steers him in that arm, but the chase is physically capped: he walks left at ~1.6 px/frame against the mushroom's 1 px/frame (after an initial 20 frames of momentum to the right), the gap narrows 93 -> 48 px, and at frame 95 he hits the screen's left edge (the screen never scrolls back, and he cannot go left of it); the mushroom then walks off (and its slot despawns, frame 124). Not labelled in the spec but measured: an extra pair of runs with the mushroom only **32 px** behind (`B1_near32`, `B2_near32`): B1 then turns back (Left 146 frames, margin 0.0063, gap 37 at the turn) and B2 as before (159 frames); **neither catches it before the screen edge** either (lost at frames 154 / 156).

## 3. Controls
- **Five shuffled brains on B2** (smell on, home off; same additions shuffled by the same rule): **eaten: 0/5**. shuf0 turns back (Left 114 frames, x min 1597) and does not catch it; shuf1-shuf4 do nothing (no Left; they walk 120 px right). **Left presses with nothing in view: 0 in all five** (no-mushroom runs, 200 frames).

So the noise is not what decides it; the geometry and the home signal are.

## Paragraph
The two scenes separate what M2 could not. **Smell alone works**: with home off the steer passes the Left threshold by 0.054 (a factor ~2,700 more margin than home-on's 2e-5), the fly moves with no walk drive at all, and he walks to an approaching mushroom and eats it 40 frames sooner than the no-smell control. **Smell against home does not**: with the home signal on, a mushroom that moves away cannot hold him; the 3-frame blip is the whole response (B1, 0/5 with noise). That matches M2's note: with SMELL_GAIN set to the crossing level at 64 px the smell is ~equal to home only at its starting distance, and any walking away drops it below. And **a chase needs room**: even the home-off fly cannot catch a retreating mushroom in 1-1 (screen edge), so "eaten" is not a possible readout for scene B as built; turning back, the steer margin and the distance chased are. Nothing here changes a rule or a gain; if the next scene should let smell beat home, that is the SMELL_GAIN / HOME_R balance for evolution (as planned), and a chase scene would need the mushroom to move away more slowly than he walks over a longer visible stretch.
