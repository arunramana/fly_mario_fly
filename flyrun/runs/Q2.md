## Paragraph
With the hum as the only sense the real JO -> GF -> TTMn route reproduces the one-point calibration on three different blocks: take-offs 18, 17 and 11 px before the block (the windows' middles are 19 and 17 px), one jump each, a coin / mushroom / coin, no spurious A. The mushroom emerges at the hit frame and, with smell off, reaches him only because he is stopped at the pipe; the "before" for M1 is therefore: no approach, eaten by contact. The controls show the hit is caused by the hum through the real wiring (HUM off, and three of five shuffles, press nothing; the other two are the known A-mashers). Not tested here: any combination with looming (the Q1 miss) - left to evolution as specified.
