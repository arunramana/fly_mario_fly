## What changed from R1
Brain: whole brain + nerve cord instead of a crop and an inset; 5 labels instead of 7 (TTMn and DNp09 labels dropped); labels chosen by trying every slot assignment and keeping the one with no leader crossing another leader or any label text (0 crossings found). Game: x4 and no text on it. Data: the scrolling traces and the knob list are gone; the meters, keys and big numbers replace them; the knob values moved to the title card.

## What did not fit / is approximate
1. **The leaders are not very short.** The brain proper is wide and flat (the top ~280 px of the panel) and the nerve cord is narrow, so the side margins only open up below the brain; the labels sit just under it (panel y ~410-570) and the leaders run 90-250 px up into the optic lobes / the centre. They cross the grey brain but never text or each other.
2. **The brain is small in height**: with the whole animal in a 544 px width the brain proper takes only ~200 px of the 960; the nerve cord is faint (few somata, dim points) and TTMn is two small red dots in it. ~130 px at the bottom are empty.
3. **JO / ORN have no soma in the dataset**: they are the schematic green clusters above the head; the "hum / smell" label points at the left JO cluster.
4. **The home dot** is outside the game as asked but sits in the first ~40 px of the data column (the column's content starts 40 px in) and at 50% opacity looks grey.
5. **The TURN bar** is nearly always pegged to the right while home is on (steer ~ +0.05 against the +0.01 marks): true, but a dull meter in arm A.
6. **The JUMP meter's scale**: TTMn reaches 0.5 in the hum arms, so the bar clips at 0.30; the press line is at one third of the bar.
7. "Giant Fiber fires" is the DNp01 0.25 crossing or the grounded A press (merged within 10 frames), as in R1; the theta shown is the max over the next 4 frames.
8. The title card has no run-specific line (no arm name); it is one card for every run.
